Meet Shaan

I'm an incoming dental student from Chicago, currently finishing undergrad at Loyola University Chicago. I started cooking out of necessity—living alone meant I had to fend for myself in the kitchen. But over time, it became a joy. As I prepare to move and live independently, I've been sharpening my skills, cooking for both myself and the people around me.

Cooking isn't just a skill to me—it's a way to share love, culture, and creativity. Welcome to my kitchen.
